Course Learning Outcomes
Students who complete this course will be able to:
1. Describe motion in one and two dimensions using vectors
2. Apply Newton's laws to systems with friction and tension
3. Use conservation of energy and momentum to solve problems
4. Analyze rotational motion and torque
