Course Description
AP Chemistry is a second-year course equivalent to a first-year college general chemistry course.
Students should expect about 5 hours of homework each week.

Learning Objectives
By the end of this course, students will be able to:
• Explain the structure of atoms and how it determines periodic trends
• Use stoichiometry to predict the amounts of reactants and products
• Design and carry out laboratory investigations safely
• Apply equilibrium and thermodynamics to predict whether a reaction occurs

Units
1. Atomic Structure and Properties
2. Molecular and Ionic Compound Structure
3. Intermolecular Forces and Properties
4. Chemical Reactions
5. Kinetics
6. Thermodynamics
7. Equilibrium
