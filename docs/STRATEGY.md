# Strategy notes (sources checked October 22, 2026)

These notes inform product focus and evidence gathering. They make no claims
about market size, users, learning gains, compliance, or how Rushd compares
to anyone; none of that has been measured yet.

## What YC asks for

From YC's [How to Apply](https://www.ycombinator.com/howtoapply),
[FAQ](https://www.ycombinator.com/faq), and [Apply](https://www.ycombinator.com/apply) pages:

- Say what you're building in the first sentence, matter-of-factly, so a
  reader could reproduce the idea. Skip marketing language; partners treat it
  as noise.
- Founders matter most: one specific, impressive thing each founder has done.
- Show insight into the problem and be upfront about obstacles and flaws.
- At least one founder should be able to build the product.
- Reapplying is common, and progress since the last application is a strong
  signal. The Winter 2027 on-time deadline listed was November 2 at 8pm PT
  (check the page; dates change).

For Rushd this means: a one-sentence description of what a student does with
it, what's actually built and live, and evidence from real students. Rushd
has not been accepted to YC, and nothing should imply otherwise.

## Frizzle's public positioning

From Frizzle's [YC company page](https://www.ycombinator.com/companies/frizzle) and its
[launch post](https://www.ycombinator.com/launches/OFP-frizzle-ai-grading-for-math-teachers):
AI grading of handwritten **math** work for **teachers**: step-by-step
grading, a class dashboard of where students struggle, and student-facing
feedback; it pursues schools and districts.

The distinct opportunity for Rushd is **student-first**: the student brings
their own courses (any subject, any school, Canvas or not), and Rushd decides
what to do next and plans their week. That's a different buyer (the
student, maybe a parent), a different moment (planning and studying, not
grading), and all subjects rather than math. Rushd should not copy Frizzle's
wording, visuals, or features, and shouldn't position against it publicly.

## Recommended first segment and outcome

- **Segment:** high school students taking at least two AP courses, at one
  school whose Canvas allows connecting (token or calendar feed). They have
  heavy, deadline-dense workloads, Canvas already holds their work, and AP
  exam dates give a fixed, shared deadline.
- **Outcome to validate:** fewer late or last-minute assignments, and test
  prep started at least a week before each test, measured from Rushd's own
  data (completion before due date, study sessions before exams) plus what
  students say in weekly check-ins. Treat any number as a result only once it
  is measured on real students.
- **How:** 10–15 interviews before building more; then the 30-day plan in
  `docs/GROWTH.md`; parents and teachers only where age-appropriate and with
  the student's knowledge.
